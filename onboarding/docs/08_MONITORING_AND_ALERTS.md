# 08 — Onboarding Monitoring and Telemetry

## 1. Metrics
- `onboarding.flow.started`: Counter of flow initiations.
- `onboarding.step.completed`: Counter of individual step completions.
- `onboarding.flow.completed`: Counter of completed flows.
- `onboarding.flow.skipped`: Counter of skipped onboarding flows.
- `onboarding.dropoff_rate`: Ratio of drop-offs per step.
